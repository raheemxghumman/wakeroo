Wakeroo is a loud alarm clock for heavy sleepers and serial snoozers. It won't stop ringing until you finish a wake-up mission, so you're actually out of bed, not back asleep.

★ 10 WAKE-UP MISSIONS, ALL FREE
Math, Typing, Shake, Memory tiles and Type your plan wake your brain up. Or make it impossible to stay in bed: scan a QR code or barcode in another room, match a photo of your sink, walk a number of steps, tap an NFC sticker, or show the camera an everyday object. Combine up to 5 missions with Pro.

★ WAKE UP TO A SCENE, NOT A BEEP
Animated wake-up scenes: Night Sky, Into Space, Minimal Black, Aurora, Ocean Dawn, WAKE UP and your own photo.

★ SOUNDS THAT GET YOU UP
12 original tones, a gentle start that builds up, a different tone each morning, or escalation to full volume if you don't respond. It rings on the phone speaker even when your earbuds are connected.

★ BUILT TO RING, EVERY TIME
Exact alarms that ring through Doze, Do Not Disturb and reboots. A one-minute test alarm, an alarm health check with step-by-step fixes for your phone, and a "Still up?" check a few minutes after you dismiss, so you can't fall back asleep.

★ YOUR MORNING, SORTED
After the alarm: your plan for the day, the weather, your streak, a mood check-in and a quote. Track your mornings with a wake-up calendar, streaks, stats and medals.

★ SLEEP BETTER
Sleep sounds (rain, ocean, white, brown and pink noise, fan) that always fade out before your alarm, a bedtime worked out from your next alarm, and gentle bedtime reminders.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no sign-up. Your alarms, missions and photos stay on your phone.

Free with one small banner, never on the alarm screen. Wakeroo Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription: up to 5 missions per alarm, every scene and tone, Harder Mode, full history and no ads.
