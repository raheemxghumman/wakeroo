# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Wakeroo is made by Qubiq Studios. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your alarms and everything about your mornings stay on your phone. There is no account, and we don't run our own servers.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you create in Wakeroo is stored only on your device:

- your alarms, missions and sound choices
- codes and NFC tag IDs you register for missions
- photos you take for the Photo spot mission or a custom wake-up scene
- your ring history, streaks, medals, mood check-ins, routine, plans and saved quotes
- your settings

We never receive any of this. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your alarms and settings in your personal Google backup so they can return on a new phone. Mission photos are excluded from that backup.

## Camera

The camera is used only while a camera mission or its setup screen is open: QR / Barcode, Photo spot and Item hunt. Images are analysed on your phone by Google ML Kit and are never uploaded. The only images saved are the reference photos you choose to take.

## Physical activity

The Steps mission uses the step sensor to count your steps while that mission is on screen. Step data is not stored or shared.

## NFC

The NFC tag mission reads the ID of the sticker you register. The ID is stored on your phone only.

## Weather

If you add a city on the Morning tab:

- the city name you search for is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service
- the city's coordinates are then sent to the Open-Meteo forecast service to get the weather

Wakeroo never asks for your location. Open-Meteo's privacy policy is at https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.

## Ads (free version only)

The free version shows one small banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interactions. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it's required, Wakeroo asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy options.

Learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying Wakeroo Pro removes ads completely.

## Purchases

Wakeroo Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Pro.

## What we don't do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics or tracking in the app.
- No selling or sharing of your personal information.
- No location access.

## Children

Wakeroo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Deleting your data

To delete everything, uninstall Wakeroo or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings › Apps › Wakeroo › Storage › Clear storage).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on its store listing, with a new date at the top.
